PCR · Operator quick guide

SET UP YOUR BROADCAST

01 · Match

Name the match, choose 1–20 teams, and enter the team names.

02 · Rules & look

Set the points formula, overlay font, text and player-state colors. Team card shades are assigned automatically.

03 · Players & Recall

Choose each team’s player count. Switch Recall on if needed and pick its thunder color.

04 · Logos

Pick a local logo to compress it automatically, or enter an online logo URL.

05 · Go live

Create the match, copy its OBS overlay link, and add that link as a Browser Source in OBS. Score and player-state changes sync live.
